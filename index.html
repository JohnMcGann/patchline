OLMC Patch Line

Select a hospital or line, confirm the number tree, then tap dial.

After the call connects, your phone holds each digit and asks before sending it — tap to send when you hear the matching menu prompt. If your phone ignores this, use “Dial main line” and press the digits yourself.
